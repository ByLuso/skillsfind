# Catalogue of Spanish Public Registries & Databases

Categorized list of Spanish open data sources for OSINT research. All listed sources are
publicly accessible (some require free registration); none require bypassing authentication
or scraping protected personal data.

## Official Gazettes & Legislation

- **BOE** (Boletín Oficial del Estado) — https://www.boe.es — national laws, decrees,
  appointments, insolvency/bankruptcy ("concurso de acreedores") notices, public auctions.
  Full-text search at https://www.boe.es/buscar/
- **BORME** (Boletín Oficial del Registro Mercantil) — https://www.boe.es/borme/ — daily
  publication of company incorporations, capital changes, director appointments/resignations,
  dissolutions, and mergers. Searchable archive back to 2009.
- **Regional gazettes** — each autonomous community publishes its own: e.g. DOGC (Catalonia),
  BOJA (Andalusia), BOCM (Madrid), BOPV (Basque Country), DOGV (Valencia). Useful for regional
  subsidies, regulations, and local appointments.

## Corporate / Business

- **Registro Mercantil Central (RMC)** — https://www.rmc.es — search company names, request
  "notas simples" and certificates (paid, but name-availability search is free).
- **Colegio de Registradores de España** — https://www.registradores.org — central portal for
  ordering official mercantile and property registry extracts.
- **CNMV** (Comisión Nacional del Mercado de Valores) — https://www.cnmv.es — registry of
  listed companies, prospectuses, significant shareholdings, insider transactions.
- **Banco de España** — https://www.bde.es — registry of regulated financial institutions,
  payment entities, and warnings about unauthorized entities.
- Commercial aggregators (private, often paywalled but useful for quick overviews): e-Informa,
  Axesor, Einforma, Infocif — repackage BORME/Registro Mercantil data with financials.

## Tax & Identification

- **Agencia Tributaria (AEAT)** — https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es — online NIF/CIF
  validation tool ("Censo de empresarios, profesionales y retenedores"); checks if a NIF/CIF
  is registered and active.

## Property / Land

- **Sede Electrónica del Catastro** — https://www1.sedecatastro.gob.es — look up cadastral
  reference, surface area, use, and construction year for any property by address or
  reference number ("consulta de datos no protegidos" requires no login). Ownership details
  require a digital certificate / Cl@ve and a legitimate interest.
- **Real estate listing portals** — Idealista (https://www.idealista.com), Fotocasa
  (https://www.fotocasa.es) — historical listing data useful for valuation context and
  sometimes contact details of sellers/agents.

## Judicial / Legal

- **CENDOJ / Poder Judicial** — https://www.poderjudicial.es — search published case law
  ("Buscador de Jurisprudencia") by court, date, parties, or keywords.
- **Registro Civil** — births, marriages, deaths; not searchable online by the public, but
  certificates can be requested by interested parties via https://sede.mjusticia.gob.es.
- **Boletín Oficial del Registro Civil / edictos judiciales** — published via BOE for
  notifications when a party cannot be located.

## Public Procurement, Subsidies & Transparency

- **Plataforma de Contratación del Sector Público** — https://contrataciondelestado.es —
  search public tenders and awarded contracts by company or contracting authority.
- **Base de Datos Nacional de Subvenciones (BDNS)** — https://www.infosubvenciones.es —
  search public grants/subsidies by recipient (person or company) and amount.
- **Portal de Transparencia** — https://transparencia.gob.es — central government
  transparency portal: senior official agendas, asset declarations, salaries.
- Each ministry, autonomous community, and large municipality typically runs its own
  transparency portal (search "`<entity name>` portal de transparencia").

## Professional Registers (Colegios Profesionales)

Spain regulates many professions through provincial "colegios" federated under a national
council. These often have searchable member directories:

- **Abogacía (lawyers)** — Consejo General de la Abogacía Española —
  https://www.abogacia.es — directory of colegios, some with member search ("censo de
  letrados").
- **Medicina (doctors)** — Consejo General de Colegios Oficiales de Médicos (CGCOM) —
  https://www.cgcom.es
- **Arquitectura** — Consejo Superior de los Colegios de Arquitectos de España (CSCAE) —
  https://www.cscae.com
- Similar councils exist for engineers, notaries (Consejo General del Notariado —
  https://www.notariado.org, with a notary directory and a free "Registro de Últimas
  Voluntades" style certificate request service), economists, etc.

## Intellectual Property

- **OEPM** (Oficina Española de Patentes y Marcas) — https://www.oepm.es — search trademarks,
  patents, designs registered in Spain.

## Vehicles / Traffic

- **DGT** (Dirección General de Tráfico) — https://www.dgt.es — "Informe de Vehículo" (paid,
  small fee) gives ITV status, insurance status, and whether a vehicle is reported stolen,
  given the plate number. Does not reveal the owner's identity to third parties.

## Press & Media Archives

- **Hemeroteca ABC** — https://www.abc.es/archivo/ — searchable digitized archive since 1903.
- **Hemeroteca La Vanguardia** — http://hemeroteca.lavanguardia.com — archive since 1881.
- **Biblioteca Nacional de España — Hemeroteca Digital** —
  http://hemerotecadigital.bne.es — aggregates many historical Spanish newspapers.

## Statistics & Demographics

- **INE** (Instituto Nacional de Estadística) — https://www.ine.es — population, business
  demographics, economic indicators by municipality/region (aggregate data only, not
  individual lookups).

## Regulatory / Compliance Notices

- **AEPD** (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos) — https://www.aepd.es — published
  sanction resolutions against companies/individuals for data protection violations,
  searchable by name.
- Sector regulators (CNMC for telecoms/energy/competition, DGSFP for insurance) publish
  sanction registers and authorized-entity lists on their respective sites.

## Domains & Internet Infrastructure

- **NIC.es / Red.es** — https://www.dominios.es — official `.es` registry; WHOIS lookups
  (note: registrant personal data is generally redacted under GDPR unless the registrant is
  an organization or has opted in).
- **RIPE NCC database** — https://apps.db.ripe.net — IP allocation and AS number lookups for
  Spanish networks (search by organization name or netname).
